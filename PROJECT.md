# Business Strategy — Website Build Pack

> **Purpose:** This file is the master business strategy and source of truth for the company website. AI coding agents should follow this strategy and must not independently change the business positioning, service priority, target customer, or credibility rules unless explicitly instructed.

## 1. Business Definition

We are building a **B2B Cloud Engineering + Managed Technology Services Company**.

The company must not be positioned as:
- a cheap website development agency
- a generic IT company
- a digital marketing agency
- a freelancer group
- a generic AI agency

### Core Proposition

> **We build, deploy, manage and support business-critical applications and cloud infrastructure.**

The company can support multiple stages of the technology lifecycle:

**Assess → Design → Build/Fix → Deploy → Monitor → Manage → Support**

---

## 2. Business Objective

The business will have two revenue engines.

### Project Revenue

Potential one-time engagements include:
- Cloud setup
- Cloud migration
- DevOps implementation
- CI/CD implementation
- Infrastructure automation
- Application development
- Application modernization
- Backup and disaster recovery implementation
- Streaming infrastructure
- Infrastructure optimization

### Recurring Revenue

Long-term engagements include:
- Managed cloud
- Managed servers and infrastructure
- Monitoring
- Maintenance
- Backup management
- DevOps support
- Application support
- Fractional engineering
- Dedicated technology team

Recurring revenue is strategically preferred.

### Preferred Commercial Journey

**Assessment → Implementation → Managed Service**

Examples:

**Cloud Assessment → Infrastructure Remediation → Monthly Managed Cloud**

**Application Development → Cloud Deployment → Monitoring → Monthly Support**

The company should not depend only on continuously acquiring one-time development projects.

## 4. Ideal Customer Profile

The ideal customer is **not** primarily someone looking for a low-cost brochure website.

A strong-fit customer is:

> A business with an existing or planned application/technology operation where technology matters to business operations, but the organization does not have every required engineering and infrastructure capability internally.

### Strong-Fit Client Types

#### Startup Without DevOps
Developers exist, but there is no dedicated infrastructure specialist.

#### Growing SaaS Company
Users and infrastructure complexity are increasing.

#### SME Undergoing Digital Transformation
The business wants to modernize legacy/manual processes through applications and cloud technology.

#### Company Already Using AWS
The company has cost, reliability, monitoring, deployment, architecture, or operational problems.

#### Agency
The agency has clients but lacks sufficient cloud, backend, DevOps, or infrastructure capability.

#### Media / Streaming Company
The company needs specialist live/streaming infrastructure expertise.

## 6. Problems We Sell Against

The company should sell solutions to business and operational problems, not simply lists of technologies.

### Infrastructure Problems
- Frequent downtime
- Unreliable servers
- Poor architecture
- Infrastructure scaling problems
- Weak monitoring
- Lack of alerting
- Configuration inconsistencies

### DevOps Problems
- Manual deployments
- No CI/CD
- Deployment failures
- Slow releases
- Environment inconsistencies
- No infrastructure automation

### Cloud Problems
- Poor AWS architecture
- Uncontrolled cloud costs
- Cloud migration difficulty
- Security configuration gaps
- Poor resource utilization
- Lack of cloud expertise

### Operations Problems
- Nobody owns infrastructure
- Backups exist but recovery is uncertain
- Incidents are reactive
- No proper monitoring
- No documentation
- No disaster recovery plan

### Software Problems
- Legacy applications
- Manual business processes
- Need for custom internal systems
- Need for APIs
- Need for customer/business portals
- Application scalability problems

### Resource Problems

A particularly strong pain point:

> **We need technical expertise, but we don't need—or can't justify—a full internal team.**

This is an important entry point for the Fractional Technology Team offer.
